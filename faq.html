Booking and basics

What is Compass Blue at Lahaina Shores?

Compass Blue at Lahaina Shores is a privately owned studio vacation rental inside Lahaina Shores Beach Resort, 475 Front Street, Lahaina, Maui, Hawaii 96761. It sleeps 3, has a full kitchen and a private lanai that faces the West Maui Mountains, and is hosted directly by its owner, Kristie.

Is Compass Blue related to Compass real estate?

No. Compass Blue at Lahaina Shores is a single vacation rental studio. It has no connection to the Compass real estate brokerage or to any property for sale.

Is Compass Blue part of Castle Resorts or the hotel rental pool at Lahaina Shores?

No. Lahaina Shores is a building of individually owned units, and Castle Resorts runs the building's front desk and its own reservations. Compass Blue is privately owned and hosted by its owner, outside any rental pool. Guests use the same building, beach, pool and hot tub.

How do I book, and is there a service fee?

The main booking button goes to Houfy, which handles the calendar and payment and does not charge guests a service fee. Booking there avoids the platform fees built into Airbnb and VRBO prices. The same studio is also listed on Airbnb and VRBO if you prefer those. Questions go through whichever of the three you use; there is no separate request form.

How many people does it sleep?

Three at most. There is a queen bed plus two Serta armchairs that each unfold into a twin bed. It is one open studio, so it is most comfortable for one or two guests; with both chairs open, floor space gets tight.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is 3:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM. Ask if you need a different time.

Is there parking?

The resort's own lot is $15 per day. Free street parking is available on Shaw Street; read the posted signs before you walk away.

Does the studio have air conditioning and WiFi?

Yes. Central air conditioning and free WiFi are included.

Is there a pool?

Yes. Lahaina Shores has an oceanfront pool and hot tub that guests of Compass Blue can use. There are restroom stalls by the pool for rinsing off and changing.

Are pets allowed?

No, pets are not allowed.

Is the bathroom private?

Yes. Many Lahaina Shores studios were opened up so the toilet and shower share a room with the sink. This one kept its walls and solid doors, so the toilet and shower are separate from the vanity.

Is Compass Blue licensed?

The view and the beach

Does the studio have an ocean view?

No, and it is not advertised as one. The lanai faces mauka, toward the West Maui Mountains, where rainbows are common, with the Lahaina Banyan Tree and a glimpse of the harbor off to the left. The ocean is on the other side of the building: take the elevator down and the sand is steps from the lobby.

Is Compass Blue on the beach?

The building is. Lahaina Shores is an oceanfront building with sand directly in front of it, so you walk from the lobby to the beach without crossing a road. It is the only resort on this stretch of beach, which keeps the sand uncrowded.

Can you see humpback whales?

In whale season, November through May with the most activity from January through March, guests watch whales breach from the beach in front of the building. Whale watching boats also leave from Lahaina Harbor, 2 to 3 blocks away.

Where do boat tours leave from?

It depends on the operator and the day. Lahaina Harbor is the one you can walk to, 2 to 3 blocks from Compass Blue. Other West Maui tours leave from Mala Wharf or from Māʻalaea Harbor, which are a drive away, and some catamarans load right off Kaanapali Beach in front of Whalers Village. All of them can come up in the same search, so check the departure location before you book.

Are there Hawaiian monk seals on the beach?

Sometimes. Hawaiian monk seals have rested on the beach in front of Lahaina Shores. They are endangered; NOAA estimates about 1,600 remain. Sightings are never guaranteed. Stay at least 50 feet away and never disturb a resting seal.

Will I see sea turtles?

Green sea turtles (honu) are seen in the water and occasionally resting on the sand in front of the building, year-round. They are protected; watch from a distance and let them be.

Are there surf lessons nearby?

Yes. Hi Surf Club gives lessons on the beach right next to Lahaina Shores, and pop-up board rentals set up there too.

Location and getting around

Where exactly is it?

475 Front Street, Lahaina, HI 96761, at the south end of Front Street in the Kaanapali area of West Maui. Whalers Village and the Kaanapali resorts are about 5 miles north by car.

How does it compare with staying at a Kaanapali resort?

It is the same coastline, about 5 miles south of the Kaanapali resort strip. Lahaina Shores is the only resort on its stretch of beach, so the sand is uncrowded. The studio has a full kitchen, which most resort rooms do not, parking is $15 a day or free on the street, and the nightly cost is far lower. Whalers Village and the Kaanapali restaurants are a short drive away when you want them.

Is Lahaina open to visitors after the 2023 fire?

Yes. Lahaina Shores is fully operating, along with the beach, the pool and the restaurants nearby. Lahaina Harbor's boat tours and excursions are running. Local vendors and long-time Lahaina shops are open at ʻUlu o Lele Marketplace and the Cannery and Keawe Hub area. Parts of historic Front Street are still being rebuilt.

How far are Lahaina Harbor and the Banyan Tree?

Both are 2 to 3 blocks away, an easy walk. The Lahaina Banyan Tree was planted in 1873.

How far is ʻUlu o Lele Marketplace?

About a mile, at Waineʻe and Papalaua Streets. You can walk or make it a short drive. It brings local retailers and restaurants together, including the Front Street Fresh food truck (breakfast burritos, smoothies, burgers, french toast).

Where can I get coffee in the morning?

Pau Bakery is in the Lahaina Shores lobby, with coffee and pastries.

Where are the closest restaurants and groceries?

Along the oceanfront walk: Old Lahaina Luau, Honu Oceanside, Star Noodle, Aloha Mixed Plate and Coco Deck. Foodland Farms and Safeway are 1.8 miles away, and Safeway or Demming Delivers can bring groceries to the studio.

How long is the drive from Kahului Airport (OGG)?

Plan on 45 to 55 minutes on Highway 30 (Honoapiilani Highway). Afternoon traffic near Lahaina can add time. Rental cars, Uber and Lyft are available at the airport.

Do I need a car?

Not for the beach, harbor, Banyan Tree or the lobby bakery. A car or rideshare helps for groceries, Kaanapali and the rest of the island.

Can a larger group stay?

The studio sleeps 3. Larger families and wedding groups often book several units in Lahaina Shores and meet on the beach at sunset. Message Kristie through Houfy, Airbnb or VRBO to coordinate.
