Start the day on your lanai facing the West Maui Mountains, then take the elevator down to an uncrowded beach. ## Your property

Sleeping. This is one open studio. The queen bed is the main bed. The two cream armchairs in the photos are Serta sleeper chairs, and each unfolds into a twin bed. The gray quilted covers in the drawers go on as mattress pads. With one chair open the room still feels roomy; with both open, floor space gets tight. It is most comfortable for 2 guests and sleeps 3 at most.

Kitchen. A full kitchen, not a kitchenette: range and oven, full-size refrigerator, microwave, dishwasher and toaster. Guests tell me nothing is missing. The granite breakfast bar seats two. The coffee machine takes Nespresso pods and K-cups; there are usually some here, but bring your favorites to be safe.

Lanai and bath. The private lanai faces the West Maui Mountains, with the Banyan Tree visible to your left. The toilet and shower have their own room with a full wall and door. Central A/C. Recently renovated, coastal design. Beach towels, chairs and umbrella provided.

The building. Beachfront, with de-sand showers, pool, hot tub and a 24-hour front desk. Pau Bakery is in the lobby for coffee and pastries. Learn to surf right here, with Hi Surf Club and pop-up rentals next door. Snorkeling is beginner-friendly; the water stays shallow a long way out.

## Guest access

The whole studio and lanai are yours, along with the resort's beach access, oceanfront pool, hot tub and de-sand showers. Please rinse off at the outdoor shower and dry off before coming through the lobby. I stay here a few times a year, so one closet is locked; the rest are fully yours.

## Other details to note

Coffee and breakfast. Pau Bakery is in our lobby. The Coffee Stop is at Lahainaluna Road and the main road, a local walk-up, food truck style; go before 10am to beat the heat. It is a flat mile on the lower road, which is less highway-like. Starbucks is 1.8 miles away. Front Street Fresh is now at ʻUlu o Lele Marketplace, with breakfast burritos, burgers, smoothies and french toast; their socials keep menus and hours up to date.

Groceries. Foodland Farms and Safeway are the closest stores. For delivery, use Safeway, Demming Delivers, or Walmart+ if you have it. Bring your first few meals and easy breakfast items if you can. The real time saver is getting outside right away instead of hunting for groceries bleary-eyed.

Packing. Skip checked luggage; pack swimsuits, sarongs and laundry pods instead. Laundry is $5.50 a load, so re-wearing costs less than baggage fees. Leave the nightclub wear and dress shoes at home. We provide quick-dry beach towels plus bath towels, though heavy beach users may want extras.

Sun. Hydrate, wear a hat, and use regular lotion sunscreen. Spray leaves a sticky residue and harms the reef.

On cancellations. Cancellations hit local workers especially hard here. Cleaners schedule turnovers ahead of time and many drive long distances with high gas prices. Even a switch to another unit in the same building can cost them the job, because they work independently of the resort and may already have taken another turnover elsewhere.

You are renting a place with natural wildlife, history and island culture. That is why people come back year after year. Mahalo for supporting and caring for the local economy.

## Neighborhood

You are at the quiet south end of Lahaina's Front Street, on the same coastline as Kaanapali but a few miles south of the resort strip. Lahaina Shores is the only resort on this stretch of beach, so the sand out front stays uncrowded. It is a different world from the packed beach in front of the Kaanapali resorts.

Lahaina Harbor and the Banyan Tree are 2 to 3 blocks away, an easy walk to whale watches, snorkel trips and sunset sails. Local vendors, shops and food trucks are at ʻUlu o Lele Marketplace, about a mile away and open 11 to 7. Old Lahaina Luau, Star Noodle, Honu Oceanside and Aloha Mixed Plate are a short drive up the shore. Whalers Village and the Kaanapali resorts are about 5 miles north for the days you want the bustle.

## Getting around

The beach, pool, bakery, surf lessons and harbor are all on foot. ʻUlu o Lele is a flat stroll, a quick Uber or a short drive. A car or rideshare is handy for groceries (1.8 miles) and Kaanapali. Kahului Airport is 45 to 55 minutes away.
